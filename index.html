Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · ID Photo AI

This policy explains what information ID Photo AI handles. The app does not ask you to create an account.

1. Photos

Photos you take or choose are processed on this device to detect a face, replace the background, and fit the selected size and file limit. They are not uploaded to our servers for that processing.

If you save a photo, a JPEG is stored in Pictures / ID Photo AI through the system photo library. The in-app library also stores a copy in the app’s private storage, together with the time it was created, the selected background, and related layout choices. Uninstalling the app removes the in-app library. Photos already saved to Pictures stay on your device until you delete them.

2. Permissions

Camera access is used only when you take a photo. Choosing an existing picture uses the system photo picker, which does not give this app access to the rest of your library. Saving a result writes a JPEG into Pictures / ID Photo AI. You can deny camera access; taking a photo will then be unavailable.

3. Region

To suggest a country or region, the app reads the mobile-network country, the SIM country, or the region in your system settings, in that order. It does not request location permission and does not use GPS. Your selected region is stored locally on this device.

4. Purchases

A 1-day pass is bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. The app stores the pass expiry time and how many saves have been used, on this device. It does not receive your card number or full payment details.

5. Advertising

Rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data under Google’s own policies. The app does not sell your information. In regions that require it, Google shows a consent message before an ad is requested. You can review those ad choices from About in this app. Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. What stays on the device

Selected region, pass status, and the in-app library stay in local app storage. There is no user account and no profile synced to our servers.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The version published at this address replaces the previous one.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the app’s store listing.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026年10月10日 · ID Photo AI

本政策说明 ID Photo AI 会处理哪些信息。本应用不要求注册账号。

1. 照片

你拍摄或选择的照片，只在本机上用于检测人脸、替换背景，并按所选尺寸和文件大小排版。不会为了完成排版而上传到我们的服务器。

保存照片时，系统相册会在“图片 / ID Photo AI”写入一张 JPEG。应用内作品库还会在应用私有目录保留一份，并记下创建时间、所选背景和相关排版选项。卸载应用会清除应用内作品库。已经保存到“图片”的照片会留在设备上，直到你自己删除。

2. 权限

相机权限只在你拍摄时使用。从已有图片里选择时，使用的是系统照片选择器，本应用不会因此读取整个相册。保存结果时，会在“图片 / ID Photo AI”写入一张 JPEG。你可以拒绝相机权限，届时将无法拍摄。

3. 地区

为了建议国家或地区，本应用会依次读取移动网络国家、SIM 卡国家，或系统设置中的地区。本应用不申请定位权限，也不使用 GPS。你选中的地区只保存在本机。

4. 购买

1 天通行证通过 Google Play 结算购买，付款由 Google 处理。本应用只在本机记录通行证到期时间和已保存次数，不会收到你的银行卡号或完整支付信息。

5. 广告

激励广告由 Google AdMob 提供。请求广告时，Google 可能按其自身政策收集设备标识、广告标识、IP 地址和广告互动数据。本应用不会出售你的信息。在需要征得同意的地区，请求广告前会先显示 Google 的同意提示。你可以在本应用的“关于”里重新查看这些广告选择。Google 的政策见 policies.google.com/privacy。

6. 留在本机的内容

所选地区、通行证状态和应用内作品库都保存在本机应用存储中。没有用户账号，也不会把个人资料同步到我们的服务器。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集他们的个人信息。

8. 变更

我们可能会更新本政策。以本网址公布的版本为准。

9. 联系

与隐私有关的问题，可以通过应用的商店页面联系。
